Where I'd take it 推奨

01 NERV Command as the system, 03 Emergency as the state. Run the app in the disciplined amber console of Concept 01 — it evolves what you already have rather than fighting it — and switch into Concept 03's red protocol only for destructive and confirmation-gated actions (your agent_delete / corpus_wipe moments). That contrast is exactly the NERV grammar: nominal is quiet, alert is unmistakable.

02 AT Field is the move if "clean" means calmer — pull it in for the reading-heavy surfaces (agent prompt, mission logs) where density hurts. The three aren't mutually exclusive; they're a system and its two edge states.
